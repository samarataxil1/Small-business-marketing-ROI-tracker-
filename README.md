# Small-business-marketing-ROI-tracker-
A FinTech application to help small-business owners track marketing expenses, customer engagement, and campaign revenue to compare financial performance.
Problem Statement:

Small-business owners often invest tons of money and time in different marketing methods, such as social media promotions and door-to-door outreach, without a simple way to compare marketing expenses with the revenue generated. This application aims to help owners make more informed marketing budget decisions.

Target Users:

Small-business owners who have been operating for approximately one to three years and manage their own marketing activities and budgets.

Planned Features:

Record marketing campaigns, platforms, dates, and expenses.

Track time invested and customer engagement.

Record completed jobs and campaign-attributed revenue.

Calculate revenue per marketing dollar and revenue-based marketing ROI.

Compare the financial performance of different marketing channels through a dashboard.

Planned Technology:

The proposed application will use a browser-based interface, a backend API, and a database. The specific technology stack will be finalized based on course requirements.

Project Status:

Phase: Proposal and planning (P1)

Application development has not started. Features and technical design may be refined during implementation.

Testing Plan:

The application will be tested for financial calculation accuracy, invalid inputs, successful data entry and retrieval, and data persistence after restarting the application.
